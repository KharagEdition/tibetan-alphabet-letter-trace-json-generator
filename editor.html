How to edit
• Click empty space: add a point to the current stroke
• Click on the path: insert a point there
• Drag points to move (kept inside the letter)
• Double-click a point: corner ⇄ curve
• Del removes the selected point, Ctrl+Z undo

Tibetan writing order: head line first (left→right), then remaining strokes top→bottom, left→right.
